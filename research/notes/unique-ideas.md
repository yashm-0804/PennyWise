## Evidence by angle

### People's beliefs about spending are wrong
- Participants predicted they'd spend substantially less in the coming week than they did, and savings goals drove the bias without changing actual spending ([Peetz & Buehler 2009](https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167209345160)) · High for the finding. It measures prediction, not recall of past spending.
- A credit union study found people underpredicted weekly spending by about $100 over five weeks ([The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/we-tend-to-underestimate-our-future-expenses-heres-one-way-to-prevent-that-189100)) · Med.
- Nearest product: Cleo's Roast Mode critiques spending on request ([Cleo](https://web.meetcleo.com/blog/maximize-your-finances-uncovering-the-best-ai-budgeting-tools), [financebuzz](https://insurance.financebuzz.com/cleo-review)) · Med. No product checking a user's own statements against transactions was found · Low-Med.

### Goals compete, and apps treat them separately
- Multi-agent finance projects exist, using specialist agents in sequence ([FinAgent AI](https://github.com/GOWDHAMSANKAR18/finagent-ai), [Unwind AI](https://www.theunwindai.com/p/build-a-multi-agent-personal-finance-coach)) · Med.
- Debate loops appear for trading research ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.27864)) · Med. No system where each savings goal is an agent competing for money was found · Low-Med.
- Exact names "Paisa Panchayat", "Money Panchayat" and "Budget Panchayat" were not found; many "Paisa" apps exist ([Paisa](https://paisa-docs.pages.dev/guides/introduction), [PaisaBuddy](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/nativebuilder-build-without-limits/the-outliers/paisabuddy-ai-money-coach-for-students)) · Low-Med. No trademark check was done.

### Future self
- MIT Future You (N=344, preregistered): reduced anxiety and negative emotions and raised future self-continuity. The study did not measure saving, and the saving link is prior research ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.12514)) · High for what it measured.
- A UCLA brief links starting from the future self with a higher likelihood of saving ([UCLA Anderson Review](https://anderson-review.ucla.edu/young-adults-with-an-ai-bot-chat-with-their-60-year-old-selves)) · Med.
- [MyFutureSelf](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6745573360) is a life-coaching app and [FutureMe AI](https://dev.to/priyanshi_pankhaniya_fd26/future-me-ai-p4h) is journaling. No finance product using a future self was found · Low-Med.

### Regret and "worth it" tracking: already crowded
- [Worth](https://www.producthunt.com/products/worth-expense-mood-journal), [Mindspend](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/mindspend), [WhySpend](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6771243456), [CalmSpend](https://app.notion.com/p/CalmSpend-3a456eabf1468009935ace6132f1c91d) and [Should I Buy This?](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6766764572) all exist (developer claims) · Med.

### Purchase decisions: exists as a hackathon project
- ["Buy or Wait?"](https://github.com/jay-2611/AI-Finance-Agent---Hackerrank-Orchestrate-Sept.-2026) simulates 90 days of cash flow for a purchase (HackerRank Orchestrate, Sept 2026) · Med.

### Indian family and friend money: partly served, weak evidence
- [WalletLoop](https://walletloop-marketing.pages.dev/features) tracks money lent versus returned, and [Ruply](https://www.producthunt.com/p/ruply) has festival envelopes (developer claims) · Med. No survey data on family money flows was found.
- Small recurring charges can drain ₹5,000–15,000 a month ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/investment/story/where-your-salary-disappears-from-rs99-to-rs10000-how-small-charges-are-draining-your-budget-528457-2026-05-01), 2026-05-01) · Med.

### Why apps fail
- Manual upkeep is the recurring complaint ([DEV](https://dev.to/hariharen/the-financial-command-center-i-built-after-years-of-failed-budgeting-attempts-3d6a)) · Low. One adviser offered budgeting apps to clients at their own cost and got virtually no interest ([Substack](https://robertpuelz.substack.com/p/no-budget-app-fix-now/comments)) · Low.
- Emotional-spending coaches exist ([Custom Money and others](https://adigitalboom.com/?p=35097)) · Low.
