I didn't type the prompt for this app's frontend. I said it out loud. 🎙️

In the video: I hold one key in Wispr Flow, describe the design the way I'd explain it to a designer friend, and Claude Code builds it. Then I tap through what came out.

What I asked for, in plain speech:
• a premium fintech feel, with a Dribbble mobile concept as the reference
• frosted glass panels over black, and one pink accent
• friendly rings and big tappable cards instead of charts
• Home as a bigger ring right in the middle of the nav

What I got back:
→ glass panels over a black canvas, with a smoky balance card and light numerals
→ a Home screen with a single box that says "Just whisper it."
→ goal rings that fill up, numbers that count up, a waveform while you talk
→ Home sitting as the centre ring in the nav

The app is PennyWise, a personal finance coach I built today at the Agentic AI Hackathon by Product Space × Wispr Flow.

It's talk-first too. Say "ATM se 10 hazaar nikale, Rohit ko 2000 udhar diye" and it turns that into ledger entries you confirm. When a big expense lands, your savings goals argue over who takes the hit, and you settle it by voice.

What surprised me: when I type, I write short, safe prompts like "make it clean and modern." When I talk, I ramble, and the ramble is where the detail lives: the reference, the feel, the thing I don't want. Wispr Flow keeps the detail and drops the "umm"s, at about 130 words a minute. Better prompt in, better UI out.
