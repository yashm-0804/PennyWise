# Wispr Flow dictation test

**What to do (about 5 minutes):** open a plain text editor, switch Flow to the language mode you'll use in the demo, and dictate each line below in your normal voice, without looking at the text too closely. Don't fix anything Flow writes. Paste the raw result into `flow-test-results.txt` in this folder, one line per phrase, with the same numbering. Screen-record it, because it also counts as build-by-voice footage.

## Amounts

1. ATM se 10 hazaar nikale
2. 5 hazaar plumber ko diye
3. Rohit ko 2000 udhar diye
4. EMI bhi kat gayi 8400
5. Laptop died, new one is 70 thousand
6. Rent 34 hazaar ho gaya January se
7. Bonus 2 lakh aaya is baar
8. Swiggy pe 450 gaye kal
9. Amit ko paanch hazaar wapas kiye
10. Dhai lakh ka deposit dena hai
11. Saadhe teen hazaar ka bill aaya
12. Netflix 649 se 799 ho gaya
13. Gym ka 1500 har mahine jaata hai
14. Dedh lakh emergency fund mein hai
15. Goa ke liye 40k chahiye

## Self-corrections and rambles

16. Kal Swiggy pe 400 gaye, nahi nahi 450
17. Goa moves to June, and if the bonus is under 50 thousand, July
18. Rahul ko jo jaata hai woh rent hai, har mahine 5000
19. ATM se 10 hazaar nikale, uss mein se 5 hazaar plumber ko, Rohit ko 2000 udhar diye, aur haan EMI bhi kat gayi 8400
20. Mera iPhone wala plan kitna pahuncha, kitna percent bacha hai, aur price badha kya

## How it's scored

Amounts in lines 1–15: at least 18 of 20 lines right overall means Hinglish stays in the demo. 15–17 means we always confirm and use digit-style speech. Under 15 means English with numbers and no Hinglish claim.
