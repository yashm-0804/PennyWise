Is the kickoff call mandatory?
Yes. Every participant must attend the kickoff call on 31 Oct at 12:30 PM IST. The problem statement is revealed on the call.
Do I need to use Wispr Flow?
Yes. The hackathon is about building with your voice, and Wispr Flow usage is 20% of your score. Download it and try it before 31 Oct.
When is the project submission deadline?
31 Oct 2026, 11:59 PM IST. Use the project submission link at the top of this page.
Can I post on LinkedIn after submitting my project?
Yes. LinkedIn streak submissions stay open until 1 Nov 2026, 11:59 PM IST.
How do LinkedIn bonus points work?
They’re extra points on top of your score out of 100. Everyone who posts and submits gets at least +6, and the posts with the most likes and comments get up to +10.
When are the results announced?
Demo Day and the winner announcement dates will be shared soon. Results will be posted on this page.
